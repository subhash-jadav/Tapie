Little tricks

  • ✨ Mood button: see the mood Tapie found (like moody · mellow · warm). Tap 🎲 Shuffle for a new look in the same mood.
  • Steer the mood: want it darker or more romantic? Pick a vibe and the design follows, still in your songs' colours.
  • Flip the tape to decorate side B, or flip the case to write your note inside.
  • Drag stickers to move them; drag the ↻ handle to resize and rotate.
  • Undo appears after every restyle, in case you liked it better before.
  • ☾ / 🎲 at the top switch to dark mode or a random colour theme, even on a tape someone sent you.

Good to know

Do I need an account?
No. When you save, you get a share link for your friend and a secret edit link for you. Keep the edit link safe: anyone who has it can change the tape.
Can I change it later?
Yes. Open your edit link, or find it under Your tapes on the Tapie home page (same browser).
Where does the music come from?
Songs play from YouTube. A few videos don't allow playing outside YouTube; Tapie skips those automatically.
On a phone and it won't start?
Some phones need one tap on the little video. Tapie shows it for you when needed.
